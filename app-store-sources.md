# App Store Release Sources

- Apple Developer Program enrollment: https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/
  - Apple supports individual and organization enrollment. Individual enrollment requires an Apple Account with two-factor authentication and legal name details; organization enrollment may require legal entity verification and a D-U-N-S Number.
- Apple App Store Connect: https://developer.apple.com/app-store-connect/
  - App Store Connect is used to upload, manage, test with TestFlight, prepare metadata, and submit apps for App Review.
- Expo iOS submission with EAS: https://docs.expo.dev/submit/ios/
  - EAS Submit uploads an iOS build to App Store Connect, where the processed build becomes available for TestFlight and can then be submitted for review.
